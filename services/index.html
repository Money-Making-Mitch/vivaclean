Our 85-point checklist, published in full

Every clean runs off this list, in the same order, and here it is in full: all 85 points, published. Most companies describe their checklist. Ours is on the page, so you can hold any visit up against it.

How to read this list. Each room type carries its own list, and every point is one thing a cleaner physically does. A three-bedroom home does not get 85 points once. The bedroom list runs in every bedroom and the bathroom list in every bathroom. Nothing below is a second name for something already counted above it.

Bathrooms

22 points
  • Floors vacuumed before washing
  • Shower and tub scrubbed and disinfected, fixtures polished dry
  • Shower glass washed inside and out
  • Tile walls washed up to hand height
  • Shower head rinsed and dried
  • Toilet bowl scrubbed out and disinfected
  • Toilet seat, lid, hinges, tank and base wiped and disinfected
  • Sink scrubbed out, faucet polished
  • The narrow strip behind the sink cleaned
  • Vanity top wiped down and disinfected
  • Counter items lifted, surface cleaned beneath, everything set back
  • Mirror polished with no streaks left
  • Cabinet and drawer faces wiped down
  • Switches and door handles wiped
  • Door and its frame wiped
  • Sill and ledge dusted, then wiped
  • Blinds dusted
  • Baseboards dusted off
  • Vent covers and radiators dusted
  • Bin emptied and wiped, fresh liner fitted
  • Bath mats and rugs vacuumed
  • Floor mopped wall to wall, under anything light enough to shift

Before We Leave

5 points
  • Final walk through the rooms against the clean you booked, with you if you are home
  • Everything returned to where it started
  • Bagged trash taken out to the curb or the building collection point
  • Our supplies and gear packed out with us
  • Lights off and AC set back when the home is empty

A deep clean adds 15 more points on top of these 85, for 100. A move-in or move-out clean adds 10 on top of that, for 110. Interior windows, inside cabinets, laundry and organizing are add-ons: you pick them and see the price before you book.

Services Not Offered

For safety reasons, we do not wet wipe light bulbs or put away dishes (we cannot guarantee correct placement of your personal items). We also do not move heavy furniture or large appliances; slide out anything you want cleaned behind before we arrive. And we clean windows from the inside only, which is why interior windows are an add-on and exterior glass is not a service we sell.
