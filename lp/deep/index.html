Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a deep clean and a standard clean?

A standard clean covers the visible surfaces: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom wipe-downs, 85 points in all. A deep clean gives the same home more than twice the hours and adds 15 points: inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, burner grates, the microwave turntable, grout scrubbed with a brush, mildew treatment on caulk lines, baseboards detail-wiped, ceiling fan blades, door tops and wall spot-cleaning. Inside cabinets and interior windows are add-ons you can pick when you book.

How much does a deep clean cost?

It depends on the size of your home and how long it has been since the last real clean. Fill in the form above and your exact flat quote lands in your inbox within 24 hours. That is the price you pay: no walkthrough estimate, and nothing added at the door.

Do I need to be there for the cleaning?

Only if you want to be. Most clients hand us a key, a door code or garage access and go about their day. When we finish, timestamped before and after photos of every room are waiting in your client portal, so you can check the oven, the grout and the baseboards from wherever you are.

How will I know the work actually got done?

After the clean you get timestamped before-and-after photos of every room in your client portal. If something is not right, tell us within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it free. That is the VivaClean Guarantee, and it covers every clean we do.

Is the inside of my cabinets included?

Not in a lived-in home. Inside cabinets and drawers is an add-on you can pick when you book, and you see the price before you pay anything. Your cabinets are full, so the time depends on what is in them, and we would rather price that honestly than guess.
