Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee your move-out cleans?

Yes. Every move clean is backed by The VivaClean Guarantee. If anything we cleaned is not right, tell us within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it free. We clean what landlords and their walkthrough checklists actually inspect: inside the appliances, cabinet and closet interiors, bathroom grout, baseboards and wall scuffs. You also get a completion checklist and timestamped photos to send your landlord. No cleaner can promise what a landlord will decide. We promise the clean, and we stand behind it in writing.

What’s included?

One clean, built for an empty home, on a 110-point checklist: inside every cabinet, drawer, closet and pantry shelf; inside the oven, fridge, freezer and dishwasher; tub, toilet and sink scrubbed, with bathroom grout scrubbed by hand; baseboards wiped room by room, plus wall scuffs, ceiling fans, door tops and cobwebs. We bring all supplies and equipment, and you get a completion checklist and timestamped photos in your client portal when we finish.

Do I need to be there for the cleaning?

No. Move cleans are built for an empty home, so most clients are not there. Leave entry instructions when you book, whether that is a door code, a lockbox, a garage code or a key spot, and we follow them exactly. If we cannot get in when we arrive, we call you right away.

How long does the service take?

It depends on the size of the home and its condition on the day. Every move clean is finished in a single visit, and most clients book us for the day after the movers finish, when we can reach every surface. Your exact price is confirmed before you book. Move dates around the first and last of the month fill up fast, so book at least a week ahead if you can.
