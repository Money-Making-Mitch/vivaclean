  • Dust hiding inside cabinets, in window tracks and on top of door frames.

…a crew that treats it like house dirt leaves a film on everything by the next morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does post-construction cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the home and how much dust the build left behind. Tell us about the project above and send a few photos when we reply, and we confirm one exact flat quote before you book. There is no walkthrough estimate and no repricing at the door. If the home has no construction dust in it, a standard or deep clean is the right booking instead, and we will tell you that honestly.

Why does construction dust need more than one pass?

Drywall and sanding dust is light enough to hang in the air while a room is cleaned. The first pass takes it off the surfaces and puts some of it back into the air, and it settles again within the hour. We work top down, let the air clear, and wipe the same surfaces a second time before the floors are finished. One fast pass looks clean for an afternoon.

What does this clean cover that a standard clean does not?

The inside of every cabinet and drawer, vacuumed and wiped. Window tracks and sills cleaned out. The tops of cabinets, doors and frames. Vents and returns. Sticker, label and tape residue, protective film on appliances, and paint specks lifted where they come off without damaging the finish.

When in the build schedule should the clean happen?

After the last trade that makes dust is out of the home. If painters, carpenters or flooring crews come back after us, their dust comes back with them and the second pass is wasted. Book it as the last thing before listing photos, the handover walkthrough or your movers, and tell us that date when you book so we hold the crew for it.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We are bonded and insured, every cleaner is background-checked, and we send certificates of insurance to builders and general contractors for their files. Ask when you book and it goes out before the clean. Invoicing can run to your company rather than a person, and the timestamped photo set in the portal serves as the completion record.
