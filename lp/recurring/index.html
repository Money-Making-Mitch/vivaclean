Frequently Asked Questions

Do I get the same cleaners every visit?

Yes. Your home gets an assigned team, so they learn how you like things done: which products, which rooms matter most, where things belong. The visit runs the same written checklist every time, which is what keeps the standard from drifting.

Do I need a deep clean before starting recurring service?

If your home has not had a professional clean in the last few months, yes. A deep clean sets the baseline, and recurring visits hold it. If your home is already in good shape, tell us in the form and we will say so honestly when we quote.

What if I need to skip or reschedule a visit?

Send us a message and we will move it. Travel, guests, a sick kid: life happens, and the schedule follows. There is no contract holding your plan in place.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Most clients give us a key, a door code or garage access and go about their day. When we finish, timestamped before and after photos of every room are waiting in your client portal, so you can check the work from wherever you are.

What if part of my home is not cleaned properly?

Tell us within 48 hours of your visit and we come back and re-clean the area free. That is the VivaClean Guarantee, and it applies to every clean we do.
