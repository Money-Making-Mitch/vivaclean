Cleaning Homes in Cedar Park

Cedar Park homes are mostly newer builds in subdivisions along 183A: Buttercup Creek, Twin Creeks, Ranch at Cypress Creek, Avery Ranch just over the line. Limestone accents out front, open kitchens inside, and two or three bathrooms that get used hard by young families. The work concentrates in the same places every visit. Kitchen counters and stovetops, bathroom glass and tile, and floors that track in trail dust from Brushy Creek.

A standard clean covers every bathroom first here: showers, tubs, glass, mirrors, toilets. Then the kitchen: counters, appliance fronts, sink, stovetop. Bedrooms and living areas get dusting, ceiling fans, and baseboards on rotation, and every floor is vacuumed and mopped. A deep clean adds the inside of the fridge and oven, interior windows, and baseboards throughout. A move-out clean empties the cabinets and drawers too, which is what a deposit walkthrough checks.

Cedar Park cleans run on weekday routes along 183A, timed around school and the commute into Austin, with Saturday mornings available. Tell us the garage code and where the dog goes during a clean. It goes in your file, and every visit after the first starts from it.

House Cleaning in Cedar Park: Questions

Do you clean in Buttercup Creek and Twin Creeks?

Yes. Cedar Park is on our regular route, including Buttercup Creek, Twin Creeks, Ranch at Cypress Creek, and the subdivisions along 183A. Enter your address on the booking page and your exact price shows right there.

Our bathrooms get heavy use. What does a standard clean cover?

Showers, tubs, glass, mirrors, toilets, sinks, and floors, every visit. In homes with kids in two or three bathrooms, that is where most of the time goes, and it is why a recurring plan beats a one-time clean here. The price does not change because a bathroom was messy.

How long does a clean take in a Cedar Park home?

Most three and four bedroom homes here take two and a half to three and a half hours for a standard clean. First visits and deep cleans run longer. Your confirmation shows the arrival window.

What do you need from us before the first clean?

An address, a way in, and the notes that matter: garage codes, pets, rooms to skip, anything fragile. Add it to the booking and it stays in your file for every visit after.

What if something is not right after a clean?

Flag it within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it at no charge. No forms, no argument. That is the guarantee on every visit.
