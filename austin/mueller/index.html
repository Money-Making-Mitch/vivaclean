Cleaning Homes in Mueller

Mueller sits on the old airport grounds northeast of downtown, and the housing shows it: porch-front houses, townhomes, and condos built in the last twenty years, with more phases still going up around Lake Park. Porches are where Mueller lives, and they track in dust, pollen, and grit from the trails and the market crowds. Most homes here are newer, so the cleaning problem is not age. It is fine construction dust in the newer phases, and porches that never stay clean.

A standard clean covers every room. Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts, microwave inside, and the floor. Bathrooms: shower and tub scrubbed, toilet, mirrors, fixtures, and the floor. Bedrooms and living areas: surfaces dusted, glass cleaned, floors vacuumed and mopped. Deep cleans add baseboards, ceiling fans, inside the oven and fridge, and the cabinet fronts and window sills where new-build dust settles. Porches and patios are extras you can add when you book.

Most Mueller homes load from rear alleys, so tell us at booking whether the cleaner should come to the front porch or the alley gate. Street parking is easy most days. Sunday mornings are the exception, when the Texas Farmers’ Market fills Branch Park and the streets around it, so we plan arrival windows around market traffic. After the clean, photos of each room land in your portal.

Questions about cleaning in Mueller, Austin

Do you clean Mueller’s newer homes, including the phases still being built? +

Yes. Newer Mueller homes collect fine construction dust long after move-in: on baseboards, inside cabinets, and on window sills. A first clean here is usually a deep clean, and we tell you that before you book, not when we arrive.

My home loads from a rear alley. Where do you park and enter? +

Wherever you tell us. Many Mueller homes have rear garages off the alley, so note the entry when you book and we use that door. Street parking is easy most days, and we schedule around the Sunday farmers market traffic at Branch Park.

Can you clean on a Sunday during the farmers market? +

Yes, with one caveat: market mornings make the Branch Park streets busy. Give us your preferred parking spot when you book and we plan the arrival window around it.

How much does a clean cost for a typical Mueller home? +

By bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage: $129 plus $25 per bedroom, $25 per bathroom, and $8 per 100 square feet. You see the exact price online before you book. Newer Mueller homes often need a deep clean first, because construction dust keeps settling for months, and that first visit is a full-price deep clean. Recurring plans after that run weekly, every two weeks, or monthly, with the discount starting on your second visit.

Do you clean porches and patios? +

Porches are not part of a standard clean. Mueller porches are half the home’s living space, and we would rather price them honestly than rush them. Add porch and patio cleaning when you book and it goes on the checklist.
