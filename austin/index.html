Our battle-hardened 85-point checklist

Every clean follows a strict protocol. We don’t just wipe surfaces; we systematically detail your home room by room. Here is the whole list — all 85 points of it.

How to read this list. Each room type carries its own list, and every point is one thing a cleaner physically does. A three-bedroom home does not get 85 points once — the bedroom list runs in every bedroom and the bathroom list in every bathroom. Nothing below is a second name for something already counted above it.

Bathrooms

22 points
  • Floor vacuumed
  • Tub, shower & fixtures scrubbed, rinsed & disinfected; faucet polished
  • Shower glass & doors cleaned, both sides
  • Shower tile cleaned to hand height
  • Shower head washed and dried
  • Toilet bowl scrubbed & disinfected
  • Toilet exterior — seat, lid, hinges, tank & base
  • Sink & faucet scrubbed, chrome polished
  • Behind the sink cleaned
  • Vanity countertop cleaned & disinfected
  • Counter items & bath mats moved, cleaned under, put back
  • Mirror cleaned streak-free
  • Vanity, cabinet & drawer fronts wiped
  • Light switches & door handles wiped
  • Door & frame wiped
  • Windowsill & ledge dusted & wiped
  • Window blinds dusted
  • Baseboards dusted
  • Radiator & vent covers dusted
  • Trash emptied, bin wiped, fresh liner in
  • Bath mat & rug vacuumed
  • Floor mopped, including under easily moved furniture
