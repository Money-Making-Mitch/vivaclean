Cleaning Homes in Zilker

Zilker is the old 78704: cottages and bungalows from the 1920s through the 1950s under big live oaks, a few streets from Barton Springs and the park, with newer builds filling in between. The cleaning realities here are old-house ones. Original hardwood and tile, cast-iron tubs, and Austin’s hard water spotting shower glass year after year. Oak shade keeps the streets cool and drops pollen and leaf litter on every porch and entry.

A standard clean covers every room. Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, appliance fronts, microwave inside, and the floor. Bathrooms: shower and tub scrubbed, toilet, mirrors, fixtures, and the floor. Bedrooms and living areas: surfaces dusted, glass cleaned, floors vacuumed and mopped. Hard-water spots on shower glass get a descaler, though glass etched by years of it will not come fully clear, and we say so up front. Deep cleans add baseboards, ceiling fans, inside the oven and fridge, and inside cabinets on move-outs.

Zilker lots are small and driveways are tight, so tell us where to park when you book. Two stretches of the year need planning. ACL takes over the park on back-to-back October weekends, and guest turnovers and pre-party cleans book out early. Late July brings the lease turnover around South Lamar, when move-out cleans fill the calendar. The rest of the year, morning and afternoon windows run on a normal route.

Questions about cleaning in Zilker, Austin

Can you clean between ACL weekends? +

Yes, and those are the busiest weeks of our year in Zilker. Book early. We do guest turnovers between the two festival weekends and full resets once the crowds leave.

Our bungalow has original tile and a cast-iron tub. What do you use on them? +

pH-neutral cleaners and microfiber. Nothing abrasive on old porcelain or original tile. Austin’s hard water leaves spots on shower glass, and we use a descaler on those. One honest limit: glass etched by years of hard water will not come perfectly clear, and we tell you that before we start.

Do you handle move-out cleans in the July lease rush? +

Yes. Late July is the peak for 78704 rentals near South Lamar, so book a week or two ahead if you can. Move-out cleans cover inside the appliances and cabinets, the details a landlord checks at walkthrough.

The oak pollen gets everywhere in spring. How do you handle it? +

With damp microfiber, not dry dusting, so the pollen stays in the cloth instead of moving around the room. Pollen tracked in from porches is worst at the entries, so we work entryways first.

How much does a clean cost for a typical Zilker home? +

By bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage: $129 plus $25 per bedroom, $25 per bathroom, and $8 per 100 square feet. Most Zilker cottages and bungalows are smaller than the Austin average, and the price reflects that.
