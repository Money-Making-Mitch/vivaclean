Cleaning Homes in Georgetown

Georgetown homes take real work to keep up. There is the historic core around the Square, with older wood floors and tall windows. There is Sun City, where most homes are one story and built for easy living. And there is the fast growing west and south side, Wolf Ranch, Berry Creek, and the new streets off Ronald Reagan Boulevard, where a lot of families are still unpacking.

VivaClean cleans homes across Georgetown. Standard cleans on a schedule, deep cleans when a home needs a reset, and move in and move out cleans for the steady stream of people arriving and leaving. One clear price, set by a simple formula, before anyone comes to your door.

Cleaning that fits Georgetown homes

Life here runs around the Williamson County Courthouse Square, school calendars, and weekends at Lake Georgetown and San Gabriel Park. The cleaning problems are local too:

  • Cedar and oak pollen settle on every flat surface, and they are at their worst in winter and spring.
  • Limestone dust from all the new building drifts in and shows on dark floors and counters.
  • Hard water leaves spots on shower glass, faucets, and sinks.
  • Big two story homes in the newer neighborhoods have stairs, game rooms, and guest rooms that still need dusting even when nobody uses them.
  • Sun City homes are one story, but the kitchens and bathrooms get daily use and need a steady hand to stay ahead of buildup.

A VivaClean standard clean covers the whole home: kitchen counters, sink, and appliance fronts; bathrooms scrubbed, including tubs, showers, toilets, and mirrors; bedrooms and living areas dusted; floors vacuumed and mopped throughout. We bring the supplies and equipment. You do not need to be home while we clean.

Our Georgetown cleaning services

Standard house cleaning. The regular clean that keeps a home in shape week to week. Book it weekly, every two weeks, or monthly.

Deep cleaning. A longer, top to bottom clean for homes that have gone a while, or before starting regular service. It covers everything in a standard clean plus the built up areas: baseboards, vents, inside the microwave and oven front detail, and the grime that collects around faucets and stove tops.

Move in and move out cleaning. Georgetown is one of the fastest growing cities in Texas, and a lot of our work here is move day work. Empty home, full clean, ready for the next owner or the landlord walkthrough. If you are moving into a new build in Wolf Ranch or Parkside, a move in clean takes the construction dust out before your furniture goes in.

Apartment and rental cleaning. For the apartment communities near Southwestern University and along Williams Drive, and for landlords turning a rental between tenants.

What a Georgetown clean costs

Our pricing is a formula, not a guess, and it is the same formula we use across Austin:

  • $129 base
  • Plus $25 for each bedroom
  • Plus $25 for each bathroom
  • Plus $8 for each 100 square feet

So a 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom home of 1,200 square feet comes to $325 for a standard clean. A deep clean is that price times 1.5, which is $487.50. A move in or move out clean is that price times 1.75, which is $568.75. Bigger homes follow the same math, and you see your exact price when you book. No walkthrough, no quote games, no upsell at the door.
