Cleaning Homes in Travis Heights

Travis Heights is a historic neighborhood in south central Austin, just south of Lady Bird Lake between South Congress Avenue and I-35. Most streets sit under mature live oaks, and the homes are a mix of bungalows and craftsman cottages from the 1920s and 1930s, careful renovations that kept the original wood floors and porches, and newer infill homes built on the same shaded lots. Streets like Alameda Drive, Travis Heights Boulevard, and Newning Avenue each have their own feel, but the homes share the same cleaning load: older kitchens and bathrooms, a lot of wood underfoot, and oak pollen and leaves that never stop coming in.

VivaClean cleans homes across Travis Heights. Standard cleans on a schedule, deep cleans when a home needs a reset, and move in and move out cleans for people arriving and leaving. One clear price, set by a simple formula, before anyone comes to your door.

Cleaning that fits Travis Heights homes

Life here runs around Travis Heights Elementary, walks to Stacy Park and Big Stacy Pool, and evenings on South Congress. The cleaning problems are local too:

  • Live oak and cedar pollen settle on every flat surface, and they are at their worst in winter and spring.
  • Leaves and trail dirt track in from Blunn Creek and Stacy Park, and they show fast on wood floors.
  • Hard water leaves spots on shower glass, faucets, and sinks.
  • Older homes have smaller kitchens and bathrooms where buildup collects fast around fixtures, tile, and grout.
  • Renovated homes mix original floors and trim with new stone and glass, and each surface needs its own care.

A VivaClean standard clean covers the whole home: kitchen counters, sink, and appliance fronts; bathrooms scrubbed, including tubs, showers, toilets, and mirrors; bedrooms and living areas dusted; floors vacuumed and mopped throughout. We bring the supplies and equipment. You do not need to be home while we clean.

Our Travis Heights cleaning services

Standard house cleaning. The regular clean that keeps a home in shape week to week. Book it weekly, every two weeks, or monthly.

Deep cleaning. A longer, top to bottom clean for homes that have gone a while, or before starting regular service. It covers everything in a standard clean plus the built up areas: baseboards, vents, inside the microwave and oven front detail, and the grime that collects around faucets and stove tops.

Move in and move out cleaning. Travis Heights homes and rentals turn over steady through the year, and a lot of our work here is move day work. Empty home, full clean, ready for the next owner or the landlord walkthrough. If you are moving into a renovated home off Alameda Drive, a move in clean takes the dust out before your furniture goes in.

Apartment and rental cleaning. For the apartments along Riverside Drive, Oltorf Street, and South Congress, and for landlords turning a rental between tenants.

What a Travis Heights clean costs

Our pricing is a formula, not a guess, and it is the same formula we use across Austin:

  • $129 base
  • Plus $25 for each bedroom
  • Plus $25 for each bathroom
  • Plus $8 for each 100 square feet

So a 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom home of 1,200 square feet comes to $325 for a standard clean. A deep clean is that price times 1.5, which is $488. A move in or move out clean is that price times 1.75, which is $569. Bigger homes follow the same math, and you see your exact price when you book. House Cleaning in Travis Heights: Questions

Do you clean across all of Travis Heights?

Yes. Travis Heights is on our regular route, including the streets off Alameda Drive, Travis Heights Boulevard, and Newning Avenue, and the homes between South Congress Avenue, Oltorf Street, and I-35. Enter your address on the booking page and your exact price shows right there.

Do you clean the historic homes here?

Yes. A lot of Travis Heights homes are bungalows and cottages from the 1920s and 1930s, many with original wood floors and older fixtures. Tell us about your floors and fixtures at booking and we plan the clean around them.

Can you get hard water spots off our shower glass?

Yes, within reason. Austin water leaves spots on glass and fixtures, and we treat them on every visit. Glass that has been etched over years will not come fully clear, and we will tell you if yours is at that point instead of scrubbing at it for an hour.

What do you need from us before the first clean?

An address, a way in, and anything we should know: gate codes, alarm codes, pets, rooms to skip. Put it in the booking notes and it stays in your file for every visit after.

What if something is not right after a clean?

Flag it within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it at no charge. That is the guarantee on every visit, first clean or fiftieth.
