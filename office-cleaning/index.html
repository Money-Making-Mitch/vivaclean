Frequently Asked Questions

What types of offices do you clean in Austin?

We clean offices of all sizes — from small startups and coworking spaces to large corporate floors. Whether you have a 500 sq ft studio office or a 10,000 sq ft multi-room suite, we'll tailor a cleaning plan to fit your space and schedule.

How often should my office be cleaned?

It depends on your team size and foot traffic. Most Austin offices benefit from weekly cleaning. High-traffic spaces like medical offices, retail showrooms, and coworking spaces often need daily or bi-weekly service. We offer one-time, daily, weekly, monthly, and custom schedules.

Are your office cleaners bonded and insured?

Yes. Every VivaClean cleaner is a W-2 employee (not a contractor), fully bonded and insured. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance, so your business is protected.

Can you clean after business hours or on weekends?

Absolutely. Most of our office clients prefer after-hours or weekend cleaning so it doesn't disrupt their workday. We'll schedule around your business hours.
