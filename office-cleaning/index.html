Frequently Asked Questions

What types of offices do you clean in Austin?

Offices of most sizes: small studios, coworking spaces, professional offices, and larger multi-room floors. If your space has restrooms, a kitchen or break room, and workstations, we can build a plan for it. Tell us about your space in the form and we will quote it within 24 hours.

How often should my office be cleaned?

It depends on headcount and foot traffic. Most Austin offices we would quote land on weekly service. High-traffic spaces such as medical offices, showrooms, and coworking floors often need daily or twice-weekly visits, and a small office can hold a monthly schedule. We will recommend a frequency in your quote and you can change it later.

Do you clean after hours?

Yes. Most office cleaning happens evenings and weekends, outside your team's working hours. Your crew arrives after your people leave, works the checklist, and locks up behind themselves.

Are your cleaners bonded and insured? Can you provide a certificate?

Yes on both. Every VivaClean cleaner has passed a background check, and the company carries general liability coverage. If your building management needs a certificate of insurance, we will provide one.

How is our price set?

Office pricing is custom, because offices differ more than homes do. Your quote is built from your square footage, how many restrooms and kitchen areas you have, how often we come, and what the checklist includes. There is no long-term contract attached to it.
