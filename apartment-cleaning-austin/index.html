Why Austin Apartment Cleaning Is Its Own Discipline

An apartment clean is not a small house clean. Your cleaner checks in at a leasing office, carries supplies across a parking lot and up two flights, then cleans a bathroom with almost no counter space and a kitchen where the oven door nearly touches the opposite cabinet. Austin apartments have their own version of the job.

Built for small spaces

Galley kitchens where the oven door hits the opposite counter. Bathrooms the size of a closet, one tub-shower combo and no linen storage. Studios in West Campus where the kitchen, the desk and the bed share one room. Our teams work room by room and keep their kit in one corner, so a 500-square-foot home never turns into an obstacle course.

We know the complexes

Garden-style complexes off Far West and MoPac. High-rises in Downtown Austin and West Campus, where the front desk clears every guest before they reach the elevator. Older duplexes and garage apartments around Hyde Park. Newer mid-rises in Mueller and along East Riverside. Each building runs access differently. Put your building's routine in your booking notes once, and it is on your account for every visit after.

Trained for Austin quirks

Hard water is the big one. Austin water leaves scale on shower glass and faucets that a quick wipe does not touch, so we clean shower glass on both sides every visit, and the deep clean adds descaling. Cedar pollen settles as grey dust on blinds and sills from December to February. And with the AC running most of the year, vent covers and returns collect dust that we dust on every visit, not once a year.

Front Desks, Door Codes, Key Handoffs: We Handle It

About half our clients aren't home when we clean. You don't have to rearrange your day around us.

How we handle keys & access

In buildings with a front desk or leasing office, we check in there and they clear us up. In garden complexes, a door code or a lockbox works. Plenty of clients leave a key with a neighbor. Put the routine in your booking notes once, and it stays on your account for every visit after.

Every building runs it differently

Some Downtown and West Campus buildings sign every guest in and escort them up. Some leasing offices want a certificate of insurance on file before any vendor sets foot inside. If your building needs one from us, tell us at booking and we will send it to your leasing office before your first visit.

Why every cleaner is vetted the same way

Every VivaClean cleaner has passed a full background check and trained on our 85-point checklist before cleaning a home alone, and the company is bonded and insured. That's who you're handing your keys to.

Apartment Cleaning FAQs

How do your cleaners handle small Austin apartments and studios?

Our teams clean Austin spaces as small as 350 square feet. We bring compact equipment, work in a fixed room-by-room sequence, and keep our supplies in one spot so a studio never turns into an obstacle course. West Campus and Downtown studios are the tightest we see, and they are routine stops for us.

Do I need to be home during my apartment cleaning?

No. Most of our Austin clients leave a key, give us a door code, or arrange access through their front desk or leasing office. Every VivaClean cleaner has passed a full background check, and the company is bonded and insured.

Can you clean apartments in buildings with no elevator?

Yes. A lot of Austin's garden-style complexes top out at three floors with no elevator, and our teams carry their supplies and equipment up as a matter of routine. Third-floor units in Far West, North Lamar and South Austin are normal stops on our routes.

How long does an apartment cleaning take?

About 2 hours for a studio, 2 and a half hours for a one-bedroom, and 3 hours for a two-bedroom with one bathroom. Your booking shows the estimated cleaning time before you book, and bigger apartments may get more than one cleaner.

How much does apartment cleaning cost in Austin?

For a standard clean, prices run on the published formula: $129 plus $25 per bedroom and $25 per bathroom, plus $8 per 100 square feet, plus tax. A two-bedroom, two-bathroom, 1,200 square foot home is $325 on that formula. Your exact price comes up online in about a minute once you enter your bedrooms, bathrooms and how often you want us. The first clean is at the one-time rate; recurring discounts start on visit two, at 20% off weekly, 15% off every two weeks and 10% off monthly.

How do I know the clean actually got done if I'm not home?

You get timestamped before-and-after photos of every room in your client portal after the visit. If a photo shows something we missed, flag it within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it at no charge.
