Why Austin Apartment Cleaning Is Its Own Discipline

A suburban cleaner has a garage to park in. In Austin, your cleaner is carrying supplies up four flights in a walk-up on Avenue B and vacuuming around furniture in a 450-square-foot studio. That's a different skill set.

Built for small spaces

Galley kitchens where the oven door hits the opposite counter. Bathrooms the size of a closet. L-shaped bathrooms where you have to mop without soaking the hallway. Our teams work room by room without cluttering your space.

Thousands of cleans, by neighborhood

More than 5,100 cleans in Downtown Austin alone, mostly studios and one-bedrooms. Over 7,400 on the Hyde Park. Over 1,600 in Hyde Park and Downtown Austin, where every historic home apartment has its own layout.

Trained for Austin quirks

Pre-war radiator covers that need dusting from the inside. The gap between a galley counter and the stove that collects more grease than people realize. Our teams know these details because they've cleaned thousands of apartments just like yours.

Front desk Buildings, Walk-Ups, Key Handoffs: We Handle It

About half our clients aren't home when we clean. You don't have to rearrange your day around us.

In front desk buildings, your concierge lets us up. In walk-ups, leave a key with a neighbor or give us a code. In newer buildings with virtual key systems, we coordinate access for you. We've been trusted with keys to over 13,000 Austin homes since 2012.

We know the buildings

We're regulars in Murray Hill and Zilker (over 4,000 cleans), the Tarrytown (3,200+) and Hyde Park and Flatiron (3,000+). Our teams know which buildings have freight elevators and which lobbies have strict sign-in rules.

Why W-2 employees only

Every VivaClean cleaner is a W-2 employee, not a gig worker from an app, with a full background check, bonded and insured. That's who you're handing your keys to.

Apartment Cleaning FAQs

How do your cleaners handle small Austin apartments and studios?

Our teams are experienced with Austin spaces as small as 350 sq ft. We use compact equipment, work in a systematic room-by-room sequence, and are trained to clean efficiently without cluttering your space. We've completed over 5,100 cleans in Downtown Austin alone — many of them studios and one-bedrooms.

Do I need to be home during my apartment cleaning?

No. Most of our Austin clients leave a key, provide a door code, or arrange access through their front desk or building super. All VivaClean cleaners are W-2 employees with full background checks, bonded and insured — so you can leave us access with confidence.

Can you clean apartments in walk-up buildings with no elevator?

Absolutely. Our teams regularly clean in 4th, 5th, and 6th floor walk-ups across the East Austin, Tarrytown, and Westlake Hills. We carry our own supplies and equipment up the stairs — no elevator needed.

How long does an apartment cleaning take?

About 2 hours for a studio, 2½ hours for a 1-bedroom and 3 hours for a 2-bedroom with one bathroom. Your booking shows the estimated cleaning time before you book, and bigger homes may get more than one cleaner.
