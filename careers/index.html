Common questions

Do I need professional cleaning experience? +
Yes. VivaClean cleaners have cleaned homes professionally, for a company or on their own. If your experience is your own house, this is not the right fit yet, and we would rather tell you that now than waste your time.
Do I bring my own supplies and equipment? +
No. VivaClean provides every supply and tool for the job, and restocks you as you run low. You bring yourself and a phone that runs our app.
Do I need a car? +
You need a reliable way to reach scheduled homes across the Austin metro, on time. Most of our cleaners drive their own vehicle, because a route can cover a lot of ground.
What does the pay look like? +
Pay is covered in your interview, not posted on this page. It depends on your experience and the routes you run, and you will have the exact numbers before you accept your first job.
How do I know what to do in each home? +
Every job carries a room-by-room checklist in our app, with the client's notes attached to it. You check off each room as you finish it and send before and after photos from your phone. The office sees the finished work without needing to call you.
