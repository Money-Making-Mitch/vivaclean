Frequently Asked Questions

Prices, booking, photo proof, and what happens if a clean misses. Straight answers, in the open.

How much does a house cleaning cost in Austin? +

Standard cleans are priced by a published formula: $129 plus $25 per bedroom and $25 per bathroom, plus $8 per 100 square feet. A two-bedroom, two-bathroom, 1,200 square foot home is $325 on that formula. Homes over 5 bedrooms or 3,500 square feet get a custom quote from our office. Your exact total is online before you book: enter your home in the booking flow and the price shows in about a minute. A deep clean is 1.5 times the standard total for the same home, and a move clean is 1.75 times the standard total, so the two-bedroom, two-bathroom, 1,200 square foot example home is $488 as a deep clean and $569 as a move clean. The full table is on our pricing page.

How do recurring discounts work? +

Your first visit is a full-price deep clean. From your second visit, weekly plans take 20% off, every-two-weeks plans 15% off, and monthly plans 10% off. The discount applies to the service portion of the price. Add-ons like inside the fridge or inside the oven are full price on every visit.

What is included in a standard clean? +

Every standard clean follows our 85-point checklist, room by room: kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas, with floors vacuumed and mopped throughout. A deep clean runs 100 points and adds the detail work: baseboards, inside appliances, and grout lines. The full checklist is on our services page.

Do I get proof the clean was done right? +

Yes. After every clean, timestamped before and after photos of each room land in your client portal. You can check the work from your phone, and the photos stay on your visit record. It is the fastest way to see exactly what got done, room by room.

What if something is not cleaned right? +

Tell us within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it free. That guarantee covers every visit: standard, deep, and move-out cleans. Send a photo through your portal or call (512) 661-7297 and we will schedule the return visit.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Rescheduling is free with enough notice, and you can move a visit from your client portal or by phone. We ask for 24 hours notice before your visit. A cancellation inside that window is $80, and a no-show is $119. You are never charged the full price of a clean that did not happen.

Do I need to provide supplies? +

No. We bring everything a typical clean needs: a vacuum, a mop, microfiber cloths, scrub brushes, and our cleaning products, which are safe around kids and pets. The one thing we do not bring is a toilet brush, so have one handy. If you want a specific product used on a surface, leave it out and tell us where it is.

Do I need to be home during the clean? +

No. About half our clients are out. Leave entry instructions when you book, whether that is a door code, a lockbox, a garage code, or a key spot, and we follow them exactly. If we cannot get in when we arrive, we call you right away.

My building needs a certificate of insurance. Can you provide one? +

Yes. Downtown towers and many apartment communities want a COI before the first visit, and we send it to your building management or leasing office the same day you ask. Tell us the building name and who should receive it when you book, and reserve the service elevator if your building requires one.

Are you bonded and insured? Are cleaners background-checked? +

Yes on both. VivaClean is bonded and insured, and every cleaner passes a national background check before cleaning a home alone. Cleaners train on our checklist and work to the same standard on every visit.

Are pets a problem? +

No. Dogs and cats are part of most Austin homes, and our products are safe around them. One of our first reviewers booked us with two pets in the house and the fur under control was the point of her review. If your pet should stay in a specific room while we clean, put it in the booking notes.

Can I skip rooms or add extras? +

Yes. Book only the rooms you want cleaned, and add extras like inside the fridge, inside the oven, interior windows, or laundry when you book. Extras are priced in the flow before you pay, never added after. Skipping a bedroom or bathroom lowers your flat rate.

Do you offer gift cards? +

Yes. Gift cards start at $25 and you pick the amount. They work on any clean we offer, including a first deep clean. You can buy one on our gift cards page.

Which areas do you serve? +

Twelve areas: Mueller, Zilker, Hyde Park, Tarrytown, Westlake, Downtown, East Austin, South Congress, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, and Lakeway. Each one has its own page with the local details, linked from our Austin areas page. Outside those areas, call (512) 661-7297 and ask. If we cannot do it right, we will say so.

How do I pay? +

By card, online. We place a hold when you book and capture the payment after your clean is done, so you are never charged for a clean that has not happened. Your exact total shows before you confirm, and it does not change when we arrive.

Do I need to tip my cleaner? +

No. Tips are never expected, and the standard of the clean does not change either way. If you want to add one, you can tip in cash or add it after the visit.
