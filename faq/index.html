Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cleaning services.

Anything I should know as a new client? +

Not a thing! Even if you've never used a cleaning service before, if you have ever ordered a pizza online or used Uber, you'll be right at home. Booking online takes less than a minute, for example. Your cleaner will handle everything. It's not uncommon for new clients to be blown away by how easy it is to use, and how awesome the value is. Having your home professionally cleaned is not only one of best feelings you can get for a hundred bucks, but is also one of the best ways to free up more time to spend doing the things you love. We're incredibly proud to provide such great benefits to so many awesome people.

What is your pricing? +

You can get a free online quote (no credit card or email required) on our services page. If we have availability, you can even schedule your entire cleaning right then and there.

Do I need to provide supplies? +

No. We bring everything needed for a typical home cleaning. That includes a professional-grade vacuum and a Swiffer, plus microfiber cloths, sponges, scrub brushes and our cleaning products, which are eco-friendly, pet-safe and child-safe. Bigger jobs like deep cleans and post-construction cleans call for different supplies, and we bring what the job needs. The only thing we don't bring is a toilet brush, so please have one handy. If you'd like us to use a specific product on certain surfaces, just let us know and tell us where it is so your cleaner can find it.

Do I need to be home? +

About half of our clients aren't home while we clean. Make sure you include accurate instructions for gaining entry to the home when scheduling online or over the phone, as lock-out fees will be incurred if we cannot gain entry.

Are pets a problem? +

We love pets. Our cleaners work around them all the time and we never have any issues. The loud noise of the vacuum does occasionally cause a scare and in cases like that we're always happy to accommodate special arrangements.

Do I need to tip my cleaner? +

Tips are always appreciated, but your cleaning service will always be great no matter what. You can tip in cash directly to your cleaner or tip online — after your cleaning is done, there will be an opportunity to tip.

Are you bonded and insured? +

We are bonded and insured. Additionally, our employees are thoroughly vetted and background checked in not only Austin, but on a national level. The security of your home and items are one of our absolute top priorities. We do not cut corners.

What method of payment do you accept? +

We accept most major credit cards and debit cards: VISA, MasterCard, AMEX, Discover. Unfortunately, we no longer accept cash or check.

I don't need bathroom/bedrooms cleaned? +

That's A-OK. Simply schedule using our minimum service (studio) and let us know when scheduling--over the phone or in instructions online--that you'd only like certain areas, such as the common areas, and not anything else. We will make sure your requests are met. Alternatively, if you need more time or something different, you may use our hourly service which is designed for exactly those situations.

How do I cancel or reschedule? +

You can do either over the phone, email, or through your account on our website.

Any cancellation fees? +

Yes. Cancellations within 24 hours of your appointment carry an $80 fee. Reschedule or cancel before then and there's no charge.

Do you clean basements and attics? +

We can't do these in our standard flatrate service but you can simply book an hourly service and request those be focused on, with detailed instructions of what exactly needs to be done. And if you underestimate the time necessary, we can always schedule a second appointment to come back.

Are offices, dens, and dining rooms included? +

They are. We treat them the same as bedrooms (when estimating time necessary) so please add an additional bedroom for each of these when scheduling online or over the phone (e.g. 2 offices, 1 den, 1 bedroom = 4 bedrooms).

What if my home needs more time? +

Our estimates typically allow for plenty of time to complete a cleaning. That said, you know your home best and if you're convinced our estimates are not correct, we encourage you to add more time on manually with a brief explanation in the instructions. Alternatively, you can always simply schedule a second cleaning if the first turns out to not be enough.

Can I make special requests? +

Of course! If you'd like us to clean something a certain way, or focus on a specific part of the home, or anything else, just let us know in the instructions box when scheduling online. Please make sure to add an additional hour for anything you believe will take 20 minutes or more to complete, to make sure we have the proper time to do it right.

Do you clean high ceilings or hard-to-reach areas? +

For safety reasons, our cleaners do not use anything larger than a stepladder. That means we're unable to clean very tall ceilings, high light fixtures, or other areas that would require a full-size ladder or that our dusters can't reach. Everything within stepladder height is fair game!
