Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in a standard cleaning?
Every cleaning follows our 85-point checklist covering kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and common areas. We dust, scrub, vacuum, mop, and sanitize all surfaces. The full list is published on our services page. We bring all supplies and equipment. You don't need to provide anything.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
No. Most of our clients give us a key or front desk access. Your cleaner is fully vetted, background-checked, bonded, and insured. We can also coordinate with your building's front desk.
What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?
Every visit is backed by The VivaClean Guarantee. If anything on your checklist was missed or isn't up to our standard, tell us within 48 hours and we'll book a free re-clean of those areas. Just reply to your confirmation email, or call or text us at (512) 661-7297. Our guarantee covers the work itself, and the way we make it right is always a free re-clean.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?
Yes. We bring all supplies and equipment to every job. We use eco-friendly, pet-safe, and child-safe cleaning products. You can also request that we use your own supplies if you prefer.
How much does a cleaning cost?
It depends on the size of your home, its condition, and how often we come. Your exact price is online before you book: enter your ZIP, answer a few questions about the home, and the price you see is the price you pay. No add-ons are waiting for you at the door.
How do recurring discounts work?
Your first visit is a full-price deep clean. From your second visit on, weekly plans take 20% off, plans every two weeks take 15% off, and monthly plans take 10% off. The discount applies to the cleaning itself. Extras you add, like inside the fridge or the oven, stay at their listed price.
