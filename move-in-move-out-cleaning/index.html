How a Move-Out Clean Protects Your Deposit

Once the keys are handed back, your landlord or their management company walks the home with a move-out checklist in hand. They:

  • Open the oven
  • Run a finger along the baseboards
  • Check the grout in the bathroom
  • Look behind the toilet
  • Look inside every closet

If they find anything, that's money off your deposit.

One missed oven or bathroom can cost you more than the whole clean.

In Austin a deposit usually equals a month of rent, which puts four figures on the outcome of one walkthrough. Texas gives your landlord 30 days after move-out to return the money or itemize what they kept, and cleaning is the deduction that shows up most, billed at whatever their cleaner charges. So we work the same list they walk with: inside the oven, the fridge and freezer shelves, the inside of every cabinet and closet, grout, baseboards, switch plates.

Every move-out clean ends with two things in your client portal: the completion checklist, and timestamped before and after photos of each room. Forward both with your new address. It will not stop a landlord who deducts on principle. What it does is remove the easy deductions, and if a charge lands anyway, you hold dated proof of the condition you left behind.

Moving In? Start Fresh, Not “Professionally Cleaned”

The lease is signed, the keys are in your hand, and at first glance the place looks clean. Then a cabinet opens on crumbs. The oven holds grease from somebody else's cooking. There is hair in the drain and a film of dust along the closet shelves. A listing can call that professionally cleaned whether a crew spent three hours on it or forty minutes with a mop.

A move-in clean runs the full move checklist before your first box lands: inside every cabinet and appliance, the grout lines, the corners a quick clean skips. You are paying Austin rent on every square foot of the place. It should start out actually clean.

Move Cleaning Questions

The VivaClean Guarantee

Not thrilled with your clean? Flag it within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it at no charge.

Can you promise I'll get my whole deposit back?

No one can promise what a landlord will do. What we promise is the clean. If we missed something, flag it within 48 hours and we come back and re-clean it at no charge.

Will a move-out cleaning help me get my security deposit back?

It is the highest-leverage step you control. Texas gives the landlord 30 days after move-out to refund the deposit or itemize what they kept, and cleaning is among the most common deductions on those lists. Our move clean covers what landlords and their walkthrough checklists inspect: inside the appliances, cabinet and closet interiors, bathroom grout, baseboards and wall scuffs. You also get a completion checklist and timestamped photos from your client portal to send along. Interior windows are an add-on at booking, if your walkthrough covers the glass.

Should I book a move-in cleaning for my new apartment?

Yes, if you can get us in before the furniture lands. Even when a listing says the home was professionally cleaned, the standard varies a lot. We regularly find residue inside cabinets, grease in ovens, hair in drains and dust in closets that a quick clean missed. A move-in clean means you are not living with the last tenant's grime.

When should I schedule my move-out cleaning?

After every piece of furniture and every belonging is out, and before your lease end date and key handoff. Most clients book us for the day after their movers finish, when we can reach every surface. Book at least a week ahead if you can. Move cleans fill up fast around the first and last of the month, and again in late July around student lease turnover.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring all supplies and equipment.

What if some furniture is still there?

A move clean is built for an empty or nearly empty home, because it covers the inside of every cabinet, drawer and closet. If the home is still mostly furnished, book a deep clean instead. Either way, our team cannot move heavy furniture or large appliances, so if you want the floor behind them cleaned, move them before we arrive.
