Historic Homes, Townhouses & Multi-Floor Homes

When most cleaning services say "house cleaning," they're thinking about a single-level ranch in the suburbs. That's not Austin. In this city, a "house" means three to five floors connected by narrow staircases, each level with its own set of rooms, its own dust patterns, and its own challenges. The parlor floor has 12-foot ceilings and ornate plaster moldings that collect dust you cant reach without a step stool. The garden level has a kitchen that hasn't been renovated since the '80s and tile grout that's been absorbing cooking grease for decades. The top floor bedrooms have sloped ceilings and radiators tucked into alcoves.

We've cleaned over 1,600 homes in Hyde Park and Downtown Austin — where the historic home stock is among the oldest and most architecturally varied in the city. In Mueller, over 1,100 cleans, mostly in classic limestone and historic home row houses where every other home has a different staircase configuration. Our teams know that you don't use the same cleaning solution on original 1890s heart pine floors that you'd use on a modern engineered hardwood. They know that plaster walls can't take the same aggressive scrubbing that drywall can. These aren't details you learn from a training manual — they come from years of hands-on experience in these specific homes.

We approach every house cleaning top-down. Start on the highest floor, work our way down each level, so dust and debris fall toward areas we haven't cleaned yet. By the time we reach the ground floor, every staircase has been vacuumed, every banister wiped, and every room on every floor has received the same thorough attention. No floor gets shortchanged because the team ran out of energy on the climb.

Built for the Scale of Austin Homes

A house cleaning isn't a one-person job. We send teams of two or three, depending on the size of your home — and we allocate the time the job actually requires. No rushing through the fourth floor because there's another appointment in 45 minutes. Our flat-rate pricing means you know exactly what you're paying before we arrive, and it's based on the real scope of work, not a generic estimate.

For townhouses with outdoor spaces — and yes, a surprising number of Austin houses have backyards, patios, or roof decks — we can include a sweep and tidy as part of the service. We also handle the little things that accumulate in houses but not apartments: entry hall mud tracked in from the garden, pet hair in stairwell carpet runners, dust on built-in bookcases that stretch from floor to ceiling. 100,000+ cleans across Austin, and houses remain some of the most rewarding work we do because the transformation is dramatic when it's done right.
