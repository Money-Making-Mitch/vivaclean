Built for how Austin houses actually get dirty

From December through February, cedar pollen settles on everything. It films porch rails and windowsills, and it tracks in on shoes and dog paws until the entry floor looks dusty two days after a clean. Austin water does the quieter damage: it is hard water off the Highland Lakes, and it leaves limescale on shower glass and faucet bases that a quick wipe will not touch. Our standard clean includes the glass on both sides and the fixtures for exactly that reason.

The rest is local too. New subdivisions out east throw off construction dust for a year after move-in. Summer humidity grows mildew along bathroom caulk lines. Half the homes we clean have a dog, and the hair collects where the vacuum rarely goes: stair runners, baseboard edges, the gap behind the couch. A house clean here is a specific job, and the checklist above is written for it.

Standard clean or deep clean?

If the grout has gone gray, the inside of the oven has never been touched, or nobody has scrubbed a baseboard in a year, start with a deep clean. It covers the same 85 points plus 15 more: inside the oven and refrigerator, grout scrubbing, baseboard detail, ceiling fan blades, and door tops. Most first-time clients start there.

After that, a standard clean on a regular schedule holds the line, and it costs less per visit. You can compare both on our deep cleaning page or see exactly what each runs on the pricing page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Austin?

A standard clean is priced by formula: $129 plus $25 per bedroom and $25 per bathroom, plus $8 per 100 square feet. A two-bedroom home with one bathroom is $204 plus $8 per 100 square feet before tax. You see your exact total online before you book, and the price you book at is the price you pay.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Your team brings everything the clean needs: vacuums, mops, and cleaning products. If you want a specific product used on your floors or counters, leave it out for us and tell us in your booking notes.

What if part of my home is not cleaned properly?

Tell us within 48 hours of your visit and we come back and re-clean the area free. That is the VivaClean Guarantee, and it applies to every clean we do.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Most clients give us a key, a door code, or garage access and go about their day. When we finish, timestamped before and after photos of every room are waiting in your client portal, so you can check the work from wherever you are.

How long will my house cleaning take?

It follows the size of the home. A studio takes about 2 hours and a six-bedroom house about 6, with most two and three-bedroom homes landing near 3 hours. We send teams of two or three cleaners to houses, and the booking flow shows your expected duration before you confirm.

Who will be cleaning my home?

Every VivaClean cleaner has passed a background check and trained on our 85-point checklist. We are bonded and insured, and we bring our own supplies on every visit.
